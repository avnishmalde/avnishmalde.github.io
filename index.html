01 — About

Turning complexity
into optimal decisions

I am a Research Scientist at Amazon and a PhD in Industrial Engineering from Clemson University, where I specialized in optimization under uncertainty. My work sits at the intersection of operations research, machine learning, and large-scale systems.

Over the years I've designed and deployed decision-support tools that operate at scale — from routing billions of dollars of freight across a national rail network to optimizing last-mile delivery jurisdictions for Amazon. My solutions consistently deliver measurable, multi-million dollar impact.

I hold two US granted patents in railroad optimization and have published in peer-reviewed journals including Annals of Operations Research. I serve on the Daniel H. Wagner Prize committee and peer-review for leading OR journals.

I am based in Bengaluru, India, with ties to New Jersey, USA.
